Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

"faceless-posting" is a private tool operated by The Hidden Glitches (the "Channel") to publish the Channel's own short videos to its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What we collect

The tool only connects to accounts owned by the operator (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok). It stores access tokens for those accounts on the operator's own computer. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers, followers or any other third party beyond what the platforms show the operator in their normal analytics.

How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload and schedule the Channel's videos and to read the Channel's own basic statistics.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings, after which the tool can no longer access the account.
